In full mode, a screenshot goes to OpenAI every 3 seconds, including chats, emails and anything else that's visible.

Tip: if I get it wrong, click This is work when I pop out.

What exactly is sent?
  • Full mode: about 500 screenshots per Pomodoro, of every display, including other people's names and messages and your second screen.
  • They're processed on OpenAI's servers under OpenAI's API data policy. Nothing is saved on this PC.
  • Privacy mode: only the active window's title, e.g. "Funny cats – YouTube". Titles can still reveal things like an email subject.
  • Password managers, private/incognito windows and banking sites are never looked at, in either mode.
  • This is work remembers that app or site (on YouTube and the like: just that one video) as work for the current task; it's no longer sent to OpenAI.
  • At work or university, check that you may share screen contents with an AI service.
  • Costs go to your own OpenAI account. In tests, full mode got 12 of 12 situations right, privacy mode 11 of 12.
